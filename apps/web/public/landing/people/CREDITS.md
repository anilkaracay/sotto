# Portrait credits

The portraits on the landing come from the inter-faces pack, https://github.com/cjdowner/interfaces
(version 1.4, 19 March 2018), 256 x 256 images downscaled to 112 x 112.

The pack's license statement:

> All avatars are licensed under the Creative Commons Zero (CC0) license. This means the pictures
> are completely free to be used for any legal purpose. The pictures are free for personal and even
> for commercial use. You can modify, copy and distribute the photos. All without asking for
> permission or setting a link to the source. So, attribution is not required. The only restriction
> is that identifiable people may not appear in a bad light or in a way that they may find
> offensive, unless they give their consent.

The originals are from Pexels, Unsplash and VisualHunt, whose licenses allow commercial use without
attribution. Sotto shows the portraits as the fictional team, accountant and auditor of a sample
company, never in a bad light.
